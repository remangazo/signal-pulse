# Cinematic Web Design Expert Skill

## Purpose
This skill is designed to create websites that feel like high-production films or interactive stories. It goes beyond "standard UI" to focus on visual impact, emotional resonance, and "Awwwards-level" aesthetics.

## Core Rules
1.  **Perspective & Depth**: Use 3D transforms and parallax layers to create a sense of depth. Think about the user's scroll as a "camera dolly" through a scene.
2.  **Typography as Identity**: Use large, bold, or specialized typography (e.g., serif displays) as a primary design element. Animate characters/words as if they were movie titles.
3.  **Visual Rhythm (The Edit)**: Focus on the "timing" between transitions. Use "fast cuts" for high energy or "slow dissolves" for luxury feel.
4.  **Glassmorphism & Lighting**: Implement advanced CSS backdrops, gradients, and "light leaks" to simulate cinematic lighting and high-end materials.
5.  **Micro-Storytelling**: Every scroll should reveal a new "frame" of a story. Use whitespace as a narrative tool to guide the focus.

## Workflows

### /hero-cinematic [title] [vibe]
Designs a legendary hero section with large-scale typography, high-impact background visuals (video or image with effects), and a dramatic entry animation.

### /scene-transition [id]
Creates a "cinematic cut" between two sections of a page, using overlays, mask-reveals, or perspective shifts instead of standard vertical scrolling.

### /text-reveal-premium [selector]
Implements a word-by-word or character-by-character reveal with specialized easing that mimics professional motion graphics.

## Visual Tokens
-   **Contrast**: High contrast for drama, or low contrast soft colors for elegance.
-   **Grain/Noise**: Subtle texture overlays for a "film" look.
-   **Overlays**: Vignettes and gradients to focus user attention.
